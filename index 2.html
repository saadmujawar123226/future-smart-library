About Our Project

Our Future Smart Library redesigns the traditional school library using ideas such as AI book recommendations, voice search, digital borrowing and reading-data dashboards. The aim is to make finding books easier, more personal and more enjoyable for every student.

Our Big Question How might we redesign a school library of the future so that it becomes a smart, inclusive, and interactive space that inspires every learner?
What makes it smart?

Our Smart Features

These are the four main ideas our team proposes for a future-ready school library.

🤖

AI Librarian

Suggests books based on a student's mood and interests, helping each learner discover a book they may enjoy.

Personalised reading
🎙️

Voice Search & “Ask the Library”

Voice search helps students look for books by speaking. A chatbot concept answers common questions about books and library services.

Easy access to information
📱

QR / RFID Borrow–Return

A proposed simulation shows how scanning a QR code or using RFID could record when a book is borrowed or returned.

Faster book tracking
📊

Digital Dashboard

Displays the number of issued books and presents graphs for the most-read genres and most popular authors.

Useful reading insights
